# Smart Diet Assistant | 智能饮食助手

## 中文

一个用于记录日常饮食、查看营养摄入和查询菜谱的桌面应用。

- **拍照识别菜品**：上传食物照片，查看 MiniCPM-V 给出的菜品候选。
- **饮食与营养记录**：记录食物及份量，查看每日热量和营养摄入。
- **多轮饮食问答**：结合个人饮食记录、食物营养数据和 300 篇菜谱，查询摄入、计算份量营养或了解菜品做法。

## English

A desktop app for tracking meals, checking nutrient intake, and finding recipes.

- **Dish recognition**: Upload a food photo to see dish suggestions from MiniCPM-V.
- **Meal and nutrition tracking**: Log foods and portions, and view daily calorie and nutrient intake.
- **Conversational Q&A**: Ask about past meals, nutrition for a given portion, or how to cook a dish. Answers can draw on your meal records, nutrition data, and a collection of 300 recipes.
